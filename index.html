Our Story

A Modern Fix For A Costly, Overlooked Problem

Wooden pallets move nearly everything — yet almost no one manages them. Kake Sky is a technology-driven company built to change that.

01 — The Problem

An invisible, expensive leak

Across cement, construction, mining and FMCG, pallets vanish on job sites, pile up as waste, and get quietly written off at 15–25% a year. It's a timber, cost and carbon problem hiding in plain sight — and most operations have no idea where their assets actually are.

02 — Our Approach

Pallets as tracked assets

We treat every pallet as a recoverable asset, not a disposable. RFID and IoT tracking, automated field recovery, and closed-loop refurbishment keep units in circulation longer — so nothing is lost to guesswork, and nothing that can be reused goes to landfill.

03 — Where We're Headed

Launching hub by hub

We're early-stage and deliberately focused: Gauteng and KwaZulu-Natal first, with a phased national rollout to follow. Right now we're onboarding a limited group of pilot partners to prove the model in the field — with the data to back it up.

What We Stand For

Principles, not promises

Transparency

Every pallet accounted for. Verifiable chain-of-custody data — no guesswork, no invented numbers.

Circular by Design

Recover, repair, redeploy, recycle. If a pallet can stay in service, it does — zero waste to landfill.

Technology-First

RFID, IoT and a live ledger replace clipboards and lost paperwork with data you can audit.

Built as Partners

We run the pilot, share the data, and earn the contract. The risk sits with us — not with you.

Kake Sky is founder-led and built by people who've lived the supply-chain problem first-hand — no legacy baggage, no bloated overheads. Just a focused team proving a better way to keep pallets in circulation.
